What is Annotating?

Don't confuse it with Note-taking!

Annotating 🖍️ Note-Taking 📝
Writing ON the text (Highlight, Underline, Margin). Writing OUTSIDE the text (Notebook, Laptop).
Goal: Interactive dialogue while reading. Goal: Summarizing after reading.

The "Yellow Disease"

Critique of Over-Highlighting:

Many students highlight entire sentences. This is wrong!

  • ❌ If everything is highlighted, nothing is important.
  • ❌ It clutters the page and makes review difficult.
  • ✅ Golden Rule: Only highlight NEW & CRITICAL info.

Case Study 2: Fiqh Text

Topic: The Wali (Guardian)

Marriage is a sacred bond in Islam... However, for a marriage to be valid, there are conditions. One of the most important is the Wali (Guardian).

Prophet ﷺ said: "No marriage is valid without a wali."

The Father is first. If unavailable, it passes to the closest Muslim male relative (Grandfather ➔ Brother). A woman cannot marry herself off.

Strategy 2: Double-Entry Journal

Used for Descriptive Texts (e.g., Havasu Canyon).

Split your paper into two columns:

From the Text (Facts) From Your Mind (Reaction)
"Villagers stare without smiling." "They seem unfriendly/scary."
"Waterfalls drop into pools." "Sounds magical/beautiful."

Benefit: Turns passive reading into active dialogue.
