Case 1: Academic Text

"The International Masters Program for Managers (IMPM) is a unique degree program that focuses on experienced managers. The program structure consists of five on-site modules that are held in different countries. Each of these modules lasts for exactly 2 weeks. The total duration of the program is 16 months. Participants are usually practice managers who are sponsored by their companies."
"Program Magister Internasional untuk Manajer (IMPM) adalah program gelar unik yang berfokus pada manajer berpengalaman. Struktur program terdiri dari lima modul di tempat yang diadakan di berbagai negara. Masing-masing modul ini berlangsung selama tepat 2 minggu. Total durasi program adalah 16 bulan. Peserta biasanya adalah manajer praktik yang disponsori oleh perusahaan mereka."

The Mistake (Yellow Disease)

Highlighting everything = Highlighting nothing.

The International Masters Program for Managers (IMPM) is a unique degree program that focuses on experienced managers. The program structure consists of five on-site modules that are held in different countries. Each of these modules lasts for exactly 2 weeks. The total duration of the program is 16 months. Participants are usually practice managers who are sponsored by their companies.

Step 1: Who is the Target?

The International Masters Program for Managers (IMPM) is a unique degree program that focuses on experienced managers.
💡 Why highlight this? Ini adalah Subjek Utama. Kita harus tahu untuk siapa program ini (bukan untuk fresh graduate, tapi manajer berpengalaman). Abaikan kata sifat umum lainnya.

Step 2: The Structure

The program structure consists of 5 on-site modules that are held in different countries. Each of these modules lasts for 2 weeks.
💡 Why highlight this? Ini adalah Fakta & Angka. Dalam ujian pilihan ganda, detail spesifik seperti jumlah modul dan durasi per modul adalah pertanyaan favorit.

Step 3: Duration & Cost

The total duration of the program is 16 months. Participants are usually practice managers who are sponsored by companies.
💡 Why highlight this? Ini adalah Logistik & Syarat. "16 bulan" adalah total waktu. "Sponsored" adalah syarat krusial (biasanya biaya tidak ditanggung sendiri).

Case 2: Cultural Text

"The groom gives the bride a necklace called 'Mangal Sutra' (blessed thread). The bride wears red and gold; the groom arrives on a white horse. The couple walks around a sacred fire seven times while making 7 promises."
"Pengantin pria memberi pengantin wanita kalung yang disebut 'Mangal Sutra' (benang suci). Pengantin wanita memakai merah dan emas; pengantin pria datang dengan kuda putih. Pasangan itu berjalan mengelilingi api suci tujuh kali sambil membuat 7 janji."

The Mistake

The groom gives the bride a necklace called 'Mangal Sutra' (blessed thread). The bride wears red and gold; the groom arrives on a white horse. The couple walks around a sacred fire seven times while making 7 promises.

Step 1: Terminology

The groom gives the bride a necklace called "Mangal Sutra" (blessed thread).
💡 Why highlight this? Ini adalah Istilah Asing (Terminologi). Kata-kata dalam bahasa asli (Sanskerta/Arab/Latin) hampir selalu penting untuk diingat definisinya.

Step 2: Symbols & Rituals

The bride wears red and gold; the groom arrives on a white horse. The couple walks around a sacred fire seven times.
💡 Why highlight this? Ini adalah Simbol Budaya. Warna (Merah/Emas), Hewan (Kuda Putih), dan Angka Ritual (7 kali) adalah ciri khas unik yang membedakan budaya ini dengan lainnya.

Case 3: Fiqh Text

"Prophet ﷺ said: 'No marriage is valid without a wali.' The Father is first. If unavailable, it passes to Grandfather then Brother. A woman cannot marry herself off."
"Nabi ﷺ bersabda: 'Tidak sah nikah tanpa wali.' Ayah adalah yang pertama. Jika tidak ada, beralih ke Kakek lalu Saudara Laki-laki. Seorang wanita tidak bisa menikahkan dirinya sendiri."

The Mistake

Prophet ﷺ said: 'No marriage is valid without a wali.' The Father is first. If unavailable, it passes to Grandfather then Brother. A woman cannot marry herself off.

Analysis: Traffic Light Method

● Evidence   ● Warning   ● Rules
Prophet ﷺ said: "No marriage is valid without a wali."

The Father is first. If unavailable, it passes to Grandfather ➔ Brother.

A woman cannot marry herself off.
💡 Why these colors?
  • Hijau (Dalil): Ini landasan hukumnya (Hadits). Wajib hafal.
  • Biru (Aturan): Ini urutan hierarki Wali. Salah urutan = Tidak sah.
  • Merah (Larangan): Peringatan keras. Jika dilanggar, pernikahan batal (Zina).
