The Cornerstone

The Thesis Statement

THESIS
The Thesis is the foundation of your entire essay. It is usually the last sentence of your Introduction. It must be an ARGUMENT, not a fact.
Tesis adalah pondasi seluruh esai Anda. Biasanya merupakan kalimat terakhir dari Pendahuluan. Tesis harus berupa ARGUMEN, bukan fakta.

Safety Hazards

What a Thesis is NOT

🚫 NO FACTS
"The sky is blue." (Cannot argue)
"Langit itu biru." (Tak bisa didebat)
🚫 NO ANNOUNCEMENTS
"This paper will discuss..."
"Makalah ini akan membahas..."
🚫 NO QUESTIONS
"Why is obesity bad?" (A thesis is an ANSWER, not a question)
"Kenapa obesitas buruk?" (Tesis adalah JAWABAN, bukan pertanyaan)
