The 5 Forms

Tools of the Trade

📝 1. Listing
Brainstorming list. Fast & random.
Daftar curah pendapat. Cepat & acak.
📦 2. Grouping
Organizing the list into categories.
Mengelompokkan daftar ke kategori.
📐 3. Outlining
Formal structure (I, A, 1).
Struktur formal (I, A, 1).
🌳 4. Mapping
Visual branches / Mind Map.
Cabang visual / Peta Pikiran.
❓ 5. Questions / Argument Map
WH-Questions or Pro/Con analysis.
Pertanyaan 5W+1H atau analisis Pro/Kontra.
