The Writing Factory

Module 11: Process vs Product

🏭
Writing is often viewed as a product (the final paper), but it is actually a process. Just like building a car or a house, you cannot jump to the end. There are exactly 5 steps you must follow to engineer a great piece of work.
Menulis sering dilihat sebagai produk (makalah akhir), padahal sebenarnya adalah proses. Seperti membangun mobil atau rumah, Anda tidak bisa langsung loncat ke akhir. Ada tepat 5 langkah yang harus diikuti untuk merekayasa karya yang hebat.
01

Pre-Writing

The Architect's Blueprint

🗺️
The Most Important Step.
Also called Planning or Brainstorming. In this stage, you choose a topic, gather ideas, and organize them. You are the Architect drawing the blueprint before laying any bricks.

Tools: Mind Maps, Lists, Formal Outlines.
Langkah Paling Penting.
Disebut juga Perencanaan atau Curah Pendapat. Di tahap ini, pilih topik, kumpulkan ide, dan atur logikanya. Anda adalah Arsitek yang menggambar cetak biru sebelum meletakkan batu bata.

Alat: Peta Pikiran, Daftar, Kerangka Formal.
02

Drafting

The Builder's Flow

🧱
The Rough Draft.
Turn your outline into sentences. The Golden Rule: Let it flow! Do not stop to fix spelling or grammar. Just get the ideas from your head onto the paper. Mistakes are okay here.
Draf Kasar.
Ubah kerangka menjadi kalimat. Aturan Emas: Biarkan mengalir! Jangan berhenti untuk memperbaiki ejaan atau tata bahasa. Pindahkan saja ide dari kepala ke kertas. Kesalahan boleh terjadi di sini.
writing ideas fast dont stop no grammar check just flow...
03

Revising

The Heavy Renovation

🔨
Focus: CONTENT & STRUCTURE.
Use the acronym ARRM:
- Add words.
- Rearrange sentences.
- Remove unnecessary parts.
- Replace weak words.
Tip: Read aloud to hear awkward phrasing.
Fokus: KONTEN & STRUKTUR.
Gunakan singkatan ARRM:
- Add (Tambah kata).
- Rearrange (Atur ulang kalimat).
- Remove (Hapus bagian tak perlu).
- Replace (Ganti kata lemah).
Tip: Baca lantang untuk mendengar frasa janggal.
Messy Draft: "Cat big. I like. It run fast very."
ARRM Fixed: "I adore the massive feline because it runs incredibly fast."
04

Editing

The Final Polish

🧼
Focus: MECHANICS (Proofreading).
Now you fix the surface errors. Check grammar, spelling, punctuation, and capitalization. Make it look professional.

Mini Game: Click the errors below to fix them!
Fokus: MEKANIK (Koreksi).
Sekarang perbaiki kesalahan permukaan. Cek tata bahasa, ejaan, tanda baca, dan huruf kapital. Buat terlihat profesional.

Mini Game: Klik kesalahan di bawah untuk memperbaikinya!
"i has a big aple."
05

Publishing

The Grand Launch

🚀
The Final Step.
Produce the final copy. Submit it to your teacher, present it to classmates, or post it online. You are sharing the best version of your ideas with the audience.
Langkah Terakhir.
Hasilkan salinan akhir. Serahkan ke guru, presentasikan ke teman, atau posting online. Anda membagikan versi terbaik ide Anda kepada audiens.
DONE! ✅
