⚠️ Module 4 Refresher: Annotation

Concept

The "Yellow Disease"

  • Problem: Highlighting too much = Learning nothing.
  • Solution: Only mark Keywords & New Info.
  • Goal: "Stop Painting, Start Thinking."
Process

The 3-Step Strategy

  • 1. Read Silently: Get the overview first.
  • 2. Read Aloud: Engage your ears (Auditory).
  • 3. Annotate: Mark key points (Last step!).
