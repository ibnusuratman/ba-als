Module 1: Dictionary & Order

  • Alphabetical Order: Sorting by 1st, 2nd, 3rd letter.

Module 2: Word Attack

  • Root Words: Finding the base meaning.
  • Prefixes: Ante- (Before), Anti- (Against), Re-, Un-.
  • Suffixes: -tion (Noun), -ous (Adj), -ly (Adv).
  • Homophones: Same sound, different spelling (New vs Knew).
✓

Module 3: Reading Strategy

  • SQ3R: Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review.
  • Surveying: Reading Titles, Headings, First/Last paragraphs.
  • Skimming: Fast reading for "General Idea".
  • Scanning: Fast reading for "Specific Facts" (Names, Dates).
