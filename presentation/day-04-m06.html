"River rafting is a challenging sport with important requirements. First, plan your trip carefully... Therefore, the river rafting company you choose should have a good safety record. "Arung jeram adalah olahraga menantang dengan syarat penting... Perusahaan harus punya rekor keamanan baik. Anda bisa pilih level 1 (tanpa pengalaman) sampai level 6 (sangat berpengalaman)... Di level 1 sungai lambat... Tiap naik level, ombak dan batu berbahaya bertambah. Selain itu butuh alat khusus: Perahu karet, pemandu terlatih, grup 6-8 orang, jaket pelampung, helm."

Strategy: Capture Context

Don't just say "It's challenging". Capture ALL 3 elements.

1. Challenging Sport.
2. Important Requirements.
3. Good Safety Record.

Strategy: Use Ranges

How to write "Level 1 to Level 6"?

Use a dash (-) for ranges!

Case 1: Final Simulation

River rafting is a challenging sport with important requirements. First, plan your trip carefully... Therefore, the river rafting company you choose should have a good safety record. You can choose river rafting trips from level 1 with no experience to level 6 for the most experienced... At level 1, the river moves slowly... At each level up, there is an increase in the number of powerful waves and dangerous rocks. In addition, river rafting requires special equipment. You ride an inflatable rubber boat with an expertly trained guide and a group of six to eight people. Everyone must wear a life jacket and a helmet and be able to use a paddle at the more difficult levels.
