Sculpting Your Writing

Module 14: The Drafting Process

Just as a sculptor starts with a rough block of clay, a writer starts with a rough draft.
Sama seperti pemahat memulai dengan bongkahan tanah liat kasar, penulis memulai dengan draf kasar.
🧱

The Rough Draft

  • It doesn't have to be perfect.
  • It is "Rough" (Kasar).
  • Expect to revisit it many times.
  • Goal: Get ideas on paper.
  • Tidak harus sempurna.
  • Sifatnya "Kasar" (Rough).
  • Bersiaplah untuk merevisinya berkali-kali.
  • Tujuan: Tuangkan ide ke kertas.
🏛️

The Paragraph

The Building Block

A group of sentences discussing ONE main idea.

Structure:
1. Topic Sentence (The Roof)
2. Supporting Details (The Pillars)
3. Concluding Sentence (The Floor)
Sekumpulan kalimat yang membahas SATU ide utama.

Struktur:
1. Kalimat Topik (Atap)
2. Detail Pendukung (Pilar)
3. Kalimat Penutup (Lantai)

Topic Sentence

Usually the first sentence. It tells the reader what the paragraph is about.
"Teachers have a difficult job."
Biasanya kalimat pertama. Memberitahu pembaca tentang apa paragraf tersebut.
"Guru memiliki pekerjaan yang sulit."
💎

The Thesis Statement

The Masterpiece

The most important sentence in your essay. It controls the entire paper.
Location: Last sentence of Introduction.
Kalimat paling penting dalam esai Anda. Mengontrol seluruh makalah.
Lokasi: Kalimat terakhir Pendahuluan.

Thesis Rules

🚫 AVOID (Hindari):
- Announcements ("I will write about...")
- Facts ("The sky is blue")
- Questions ("Why is it bad?")
- Pengumuman ("Saya akan menulis...")
- Fakta ("Langit itu biru")
- Pertanyaan ("Kenapa itu buruk?")
✅ DO (Lakukan):
- Make an Argument.
- Be Specific.
- Take a Stance.
- Buat Argumen.
- Jadilah Spesifik.
- Ambil Sikap.

Thesis Examples

Weak: "Vegetarians live longer." (Fact)

Strong: "A balanced vegetarian diet provides significant health benefits and Americans would benefit from adopting it." (Argument)
Lemah: "Vegetarian hidup lebih lama." (Fakta)

Kuat: "Pola makan vegetarian yang seimbang memberikan manfaat kesehatan signifikan dan orang Amerika akan untung jika menerapkannya." (Argumen)
🎨

Review

1. Drafting: Don't be perfect, just write.
2. Paragraphs: Focus on one idea.
3. Thesis: Make a strong argument.

Now, pick up your chisel and start sculpting!
1. Drafting: Jangan sempurna, tulis saja.
2. Paragraf: Fokus pada satu ide.
3. Tesis: Buat argumen yang kuat.

Sekarang, ambil pahatmu dan mulailah memahat!
