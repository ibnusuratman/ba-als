The Academic Detective

Crime: Plagiarism

🔍
Plagiarism is stealing someone else's work. It can be intentional or unintentional. For example, copying a sentence word-for-word without quotation marks is an offense.

Verdict: Whenever you copy exact words, you MUST use quotation marks.
Plagiarisme adalah mencuri karya orang lain. Bisa disengaja atau tidak. Contohnya, menyalin kalimat kata demi kata tanpa tanda kutip adalah pelanggaran.

Vonis: Kapan pun Anda menyalin kata persis, Anda HARUS pakai tanda kutip.

Defense Strategy

Video 10b: Two Shields

🛡️
To avoid this offense, rely on two methods:

1. Direct Quoting: Using exact words inside "quotation marks".
2. Paraphrasing: Rewriting ideas using your own words and structure.
Untuk menghindari pelanggaran ini, gunakan dua metode:

1. Kutipan Langsung: Gunakan kata persis di dalam "tanda kutip".
2. Parafrasa: Tulis ulang ide menggunakan kata-kata dan struktur sendiri.

The 3 Laws

Video 10c: Strict Criteria

📜
A successful paraphrase must meet 3 criteria:

1. New Vocabulary: Use synonyms.
2. New Structure: Change grammar completely.
3. Same Meaning: No new opinions, no missing facts.
Parafrasa sukses harus memenuhi 3 kriteria:

1. Kosakata Baru: Gunakan sinonim.
2. Struktur Baru: Ubah tata bahasa total.
3. Makna Sama: Jangan tambah opini, jangan buang fakta.

The Mechanic's Lab

Video 10d: How to Rewrite?

Manipulate the grammar:
- Change Active to Passive voice.
- Change Parts of Speech (Noun → Verb).
- Combine short sentences.
Manipulasi tata bahasa:
- Ubah Aktif ke Pasif.
- Ubah Kelas Kata (Benda → Kerja).
- Gabungkan kalimat pendek.
Input: "The police caught the thief."

Investigation Case

Havasu Canyon Analysis

ORIGINAL (Asli):

"Havasu Canyon is in a remote area. It is 10 or 15 miles from the nearest highway."
"Ngarai Havasu berada di daerah terpencil. Jaraknya 10 atau 15 mil dari jalan raya terdekat."
PARAPHRASE (Hasil):

"The location of Havasu Canyon is highly isolated, situated approximately a dozen miles away from any major road."
"Lokasi Ngarai Havasu sangat terisolasi, terletak sekitar belasan mil jauhnya dari jalan utama mana pun."
