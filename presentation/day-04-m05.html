Module 5: Note-Taking

From Book to Brain

"Stop Painting, Start Thinking."

Rule 1: Specific Titles

A specific title helps your brain recall the content.

❌ "Physical Activity"
✅ "Benefits of Physical Activity"

Rule 2: The Filter

Stop writing full sentences! Remove "Filler Words".

"One of the best ways is to draw up a weekly schedule."

⬇️

"Weekly Schedule"

Rule 3: Use Symbols

Save time with abbreviations.

w/ (with)  •  b/c (because)  •  ➔ (leads to)  •  ↑ (increase)

Case 1: IMPM (Input)

"Although managers cannot be created in a classroom... we accept only practicing managers... sponsored by their companies. We ask companies to send 4 or 5 participants... The IMPM runs with 5 on-site modules of approximately 2 weeks each, spread over 16 months."

"Meskipun manajer tidak bisa diciptakan di kelas... kami hanya menerima manajer praktik... disponsori perusahaan. Kami minta perusahaan kirim 4-5 peserta... IMPM berjalan 5 modul, durasi 2 minggu, selama 16 bulan."

Step 1: Specific Title

Step 2: Filter & Nuance

Crucial Detail: Intelligence vs Proven Records.

Companies look for: "Strong intellectual abilities" AND "Proven records".

Note: Intelligence alone is NOT an indicator.

Step 3: The Numbers

  • 5 Modules (On-site)
  • 2 Weeks (Duration)
  • 16 Months (Total)

Case 1: Final Simulation

TEXTBOOK ...we accept only practicing managers... sponsored by their companies... send 4 or 5 participants... 5 on-site modules... 2 weeks each... spread over 16 months.

Case 2: Hindu Wedding (Input)

"In India, traditional Hindu weddings are usually lavish affairs... The groom gives her a necklace called Mangal Sutra... He usually arrives in a white car or on a white horse, but he sometimes rides a white elephant..."

"Di India, pernikahan Hindu biasanya acara mewah... Pria memberi kalung Mangal Sutra... Dia datang dengan mobil putih atau kuda putih, kadang gajah putih..."

Step 1: Specific Title

Step 2: Atmosphere

Start by capturing the mood.

STYLE: LAVISH AFFAIRS

(Acara Mewah)

Step 3: Capture All Options

Don't just write "Car". Write ALL variations.

Transport: White Car / White Horse / White Elephant

Case 2: Final Simulation

...usually lavish affairs... gives Mangal Sutra... arrives in a white car or on a white horse... sometimes white elephant.
NOTEBOOK

Case 3: Havasu Canyon (Input)

"There is a Canyon in Northern Arizona... called Havasu Canyon... It is not easy to get there... 10 or 15 miles from the nearest road. You have to hike... or hire a guide to take you on horseback... The village is a quiet place... villagers do not smile."

"Ada Ngarai di Arizona Utara... Havasu... Tidak mudah dicapai... 10-15 mil dari jalan. Harus mendaki/kuda... Desa tenang... penduduk tidak senyum."

Step 1: Specific Title

Step 2: Filter Description

Turn long sentences into punchy phrases.

"The villagers stand in doorways and they do not smile."

⬇️

"Villagers: Unsmiling"

Step 3: Numbered List

Use lists for travel guides.

  1. Location: North Arizona.
  2. Access: Hike / Horse.
  3. Atmosphere: Quiet.

Case 3: Final Simulation

...in Northern Arizona... 10 or 15 miles from road... hike or ride a horse... village is quiet... villagers do not smile.
NOTEBOOK

Case 4: The Wali (Input)

"A woman cannot marry herself off... No marriage is valid without a Wali... Usually the Wali is the father... If unavailable, it passes to Grandfather... A woman cannot appoint her own Wali... This false practice leads to bad consequences: The appointed Wali is usually unfit... leading to Khalwat and major sins."

"Wanita tidak bisa menikahkan diri sendiri... Tidak sah tanpa Wali... Biasanya Ayah... jika tidak ada ke Kakek... Wanita tidak bisa menunjuk Wali sendiri... Ini menyebabkan konsekuensi buruk: Wali tidak layak... mengarah ke Khalwat dan dosa besar."

Step 1: Specific Title

Step 2: Cause & Effect

Warning: Self-Appointment leads to Sin.

CAUSE:
Self-Appointment
➔
EFFECT:
Unfit / Khalwat / Sin

Step 3: Hierarchy

Use arrows for order.

Father ➔ Grandfather ➔ Brother

Case 4: Final Simulation

...cannot marry herself... No marriage is valid without a Wali... Father... Grandfather... false practice leads to Khalwat and major sins.
