Module 5

"From Book to Brain"

What is Note-Taking?

It is NOT copying the book.

  • ❌ Copying: Moving words from page to paper without thinking.
  • ✅ Note-Taking: Filtering, organizing, and synthesizing information.

Rule 1: Specific Titles

A specific title helps your brain recall the content.

❌ Vague (Bad) ✅ Specific (Good)
"Physical Activity" "Benefits of Physical Activity"
"Manager Training" "IMPM Selection Criteria"

(Source: Module 5a, Page 1)

Rule 2: The Filter

Stop writing full sentences! Remove "Filler Words".

Original: "One of the best ways is to draw up a weekly schedule."

⬇️

Notes: "Weekly Schedule"

(Source: Module 5a, Page 1)

Rule 3: Use Symbols

Save time with abbreviations.

w/ = with

b/c = because

vs = against

➔ = leads to / causes

↑ = increases

↓ = decreases

Case 1: Reading

READ FIRST
"Making time for extra study needs creativity... If you travel by a slow train, you have at least three hours of study time... Healthy ready-meals from the supermarket save you time over a hot stove... One of the best ways is to draw up a weekly schedule... Reduce your sleep gradually... Your study program should have time for relaxation..."
"Meluangkan waktu tambahan untuk belajar membutuhkan kreativitas... Jika Anda bepergian dengan kereta lambat, setidaknya Anda memiliki waktu belajar selama tiga jam... Makanan sehat siap saji dari supermarket menghemat waktu Anda di depan kompor... Salah satu cara terbaik adalah menyusun jadwal mingguan... Kurangi waktu tidur secara bertahap... Program belajar Anda harus memiliki waktu untuk bersantai..."

Case 1: The Filter

Remove the junk, keep the gold.

TEXTBOOK
"Making time for extra study needs creativity... If you travel by a slow train, you have at least three hours... Healthy ready-meals from the supermarket save you time... One of the best ways is to draw up a weekly schedule... Reduce your sleep gradually... have time for relaxation...".
NOTEBOOK

Case 2: Reading

READ FIRST
"The IMPM runs with five on-site modules of approximately 2 weeks each, spread over 16 months... Participants are usually practice managers who are sponsored by their companies."
"IMPM berjalan dengan lima modul di tempat yang masing-masing berdurasi sekitar 2 minggu, tersebar selama 16 bulan... Peserta biasanya adalah manajer praktik yang disponsori oleh perusahaan mereka."

Case 2: The Numbers

Accuracy is key for Academic Texts.

TEXTBOOK
"The IMPM focuses on experienced managers. The program structure consists of 5 on-site modules held in different countries. Each module lasts for 2 weeks. The total duration is 16 months. Participants are sponsored by their companies."
NOTEBOOK

READ FIRST

"Pengantin pria memberinya kalung manik-manik hitam yang disebut mangal sutra... Dia biasanya tiba dengan mobil putih atau menunggang kuda putih, tetapi terkadang dia menunggang gajah putih..."

Case 3: Inclusiveness

Don't miss the variations!

TEXTBOOK
"The groom gives a necklace called Mangal Sutra. The bride wears red and gold. The groom arrives in a white car OR on a white horse, but sometimes a white elephant."
NOTEBOOK

Case 4: The Organizer

Turn messy paragraphs into structured lists.

TEXTBOOK
"There is a Canyon in Northern Arizona... 10 or 15 miles from the nearest road. You have to hike or ride a horse. The village is quiet. Dogs sleep in the street. Villagers do not smile."
